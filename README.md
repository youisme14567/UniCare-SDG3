##Project Description
UniWell is a applicaton for university students to track their daily health haibits including water intake, sleep, exercise, meals. The app supports SDG3 by promoting good health and well-being through the app, to help students build a healthier daily routines.

## SDLC Overview
We planned the app features and requirements based on SDG3, designed wireframes and full mockup in figma, tested all screen flows and buttons in the prototype, and evaluated the design to ensure it meets the project requirements.
